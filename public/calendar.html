How this works

What the demo does, and what a live build would need.

The demonstration is built to be used rather than read, so here is what happens at each step.

Availability

Sample dates, generated daily

Four weeks of slots are generated from today's date, with Mondays closed and a sample private hire on Thursdays. Nothing is read from a booking system, because there is not one behind this page.

Booking

Checked, then discarded

A booking request is validated exactly as a live request would be, then dropped: no reservation row, no contact, no email. You get a reference beginning DEMO, and nothing exists behind it.

Live build

What a real venue would add

Its own slots, closures and events in the database, a way to take a deposit and confirm the date, confirmation emails, staff access to the bookings list, and a published address and contact details.
