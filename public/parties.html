The demo flow

How a demo booking behaves.

The booking flow on this site is a walk-through. It shows the steps a real party booking would take, and stops before anything is held or charged.

  • Choose a sample package and an estimate from the calculator below.
  • Send the demo enquiry with a preferred date and a number of children.
  • The enquiry is validated and then discarded. Nothing is stored and no reply is sent.
  • No date is held, no reference is issued and no payment is taken.
What a real booking flow would need

A live version would check availability against a real calendar, take a payment, issue a booking reference and send a confirmation to the person who booked. None of that is part of this demonstration.

  • An availability source that can be queried by date and party size.
  • A payment step and a booking record to hold against the date.
  • Confirmation and reminder messages, and a way to amend a booking.
