Soft play area

A soft play area for smaller children.

Our soft play corner has entry prices by age band, house rules for the room, and seating for parents alongside the play area.

Pricing

Prices for a session.

Entry is charged per child for the session, and babies under two are welcome in the mat area at no charge.

Age band

Under 8 months

£2.50

Entry price for babies, with a mat area set apart from the rest of the room.

Age band

9 months to 6 years

£5

Entry price for the main play area, per child for the session.

Session

Session length

60 min

Session length, and a short reset of the room between sessions.

Layout

How the area is arranged.

We lay the area out in two parts: a mat area for the under-fives, and the main play area for the older ones.

  • Under 8 months in the mat area, 9 months to 6 years in the main play room.
  • 60 minutes, with the room reset between sessions.
  • How many children we take in each session.

House rules

Four rules for the play area.

The rules we ask everyone to keep to, so the room stays a calm place for the little ones.

Book a party See the menu
  • Socks on in the play area.
  • No food or drink inside the play area.
  • An adult stays with each child in the room.
  • No running in the room.
Seating for parents

Seating for parents

Parent seating is part of the play area rather than a separate room, so you can keep the tables and the play space in one view.

  • Tables and chairs along the edge of the play area, in sight of the room.
  • Drinks and food from the café menu, served to the table.
  • A quieter corner with smaller chairs.
