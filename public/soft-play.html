Sample layout

How the sample area is arranged.

The demonstration lays the area out in two parts, so the page can show a price for each age band and describe where each group would be.

  • Under 8 months in the mat area, 9 months to 6 years in the main play room.

Seating for parents

Seating for parents is part of the sample layout.

The demonstration treats parent seating as part of the play area rather than a separate room, so a visitor can see the tables and the play space in one view.
